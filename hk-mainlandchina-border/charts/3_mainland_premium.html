The mainland premium: how much more does Hong Kong trade with the mainland than gravity predicts?

Estimated each year from ln(trade) = a + b·ln(GDP) − d·ln(distance) + γ·Mainland + Macau dummy. The line shows eγ: a value of 3 means three times the trade a partner of the mainland's size and distance would normally have. Shaded bands are 95% confidence intervals.

Values above the dotted line at 1 mean extra trade; below 1 would mean a border penalty. Log scale. Robust (HC1) standard errors.
Data: Yotov et al. (2016), An Advanced Guide to Trade Policy Analysis, WTO/UNCTAD (manufacturing trade, 69 economies); World Bank GDP. Code and data in the project repository.
