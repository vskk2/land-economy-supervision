How big is the Hong Kong–mainland border, compared with McCallum's US–Canada border?

Each line shows how many times more an economy trades with itself than across that border, after controlling for distance, a shared border, a common language and colonial ties. McCallum (1995) found about 22 for Canada and the US (1988 data). Higher means a thicker border; 1 would mean the border makes no difference.

Structural gravity, estimated by PPML each year with exporter and importer fixed effects and domestic trade flows (Yotov et al., 2016, ch. 1). Border factor = e−(international + pair-specific coefficient). 95% CIs (delta method, pair-clustered) shown on hover. Log scale.
Data: Yotov et al. (2016), An Advanced Guide to Trade Policy Analysis, WTO/UNCTAD (manufacturing trade, 69 economies); World Bank GDP. Code and data in the project repository.
