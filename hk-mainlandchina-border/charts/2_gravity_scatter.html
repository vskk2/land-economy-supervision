Hong Kong's trade and distance: where does the mainland sit?

Each bubble is a trading partner. The y-axis removes the effect of partner size (the same distance-decay idea as the Munich wage-shock chart in Lectures 5–6); bubbles above the line trade more than gravity predicts. Bubble area is proportional to trade value.

y = ln(trade) − b·ln(partner GDP), with b estimated each year. Line: fitted distance effect. Distance is population-weighted (CEPII).
Data: Yotov et al. (2016), An Advanced Guide to Trade Policy Analysis, WTO/UNCTAD (manufacturing trade, 69 economies); World Bank GDP. Code and data in the project repository.
