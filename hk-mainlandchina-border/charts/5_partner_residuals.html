Which partners trade more, or less, with Hong Kong than gravity predicts?

Actual trade divided by the trade a plain gravity model (partner GDP and distance only) would predict. Showing the 12 partners furthest above and below the prediction, plus the mainland and Macau. Is the mainland unusual, or one of many outliers?

Values above 1 mean more trade than predicted (log scale). Predictions exclude the mainland and Macau dummies.
Data: Yotov et al. (2016), An Advanced Guide to Trade Policy Analysis, WTO/UNCTAD (manufacturing trade, 69 economies); World Bank GDP. Code and data in the project repository.
